# Venture Charter — Productized AI Service

## What this venture is

A fixed-scope, fixed-price, subscription service for one specific niche — recurring
deliverables such as reports, data cleanup, or content operations — produced by an
AI-first pipeline with human review before anything reaches the client. Clients buy
an outcome at a flat monthly price, not hours.

## Why the platform is doing it

Ranked #2 on the opportunity scoreboard (80/100): high automation ceiling (designed at
rung 3 of the automation ladder from day one), naturally recurring revenue, fast to
first customer, and broad current demand. It is the platform's recurring-revenue play,
sharing its content/automation toolchain with Digital Products Studio.

## Owner and stage

- **Owner:** Troy
- **Current stage:** 1 — Validate
- **Structure:** Internal line of business

## Success looks like

- 3 paying clients at the target monthly price within 60 days of starting outreach.
- Gross margin ≥ 50% after counting human review time honestly.
- Each delivery cycle's human time falling month over month as the pipeline hardens.

## Kill / rethink criteria

- Fewer than 3 paying clients after 60 days of active outreach → the niche or offer
  is wrong; rethink scope/niche before building more pipeline.
- Margin below 50% after three months because human review won't shrink → the niche's
  error tolerance is too low for an AI-first pipeline; change niche or wind down.
