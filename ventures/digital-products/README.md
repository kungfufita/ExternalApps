# Venture Charter — Digital Products Studio

## What this venture is

Digital Products Studio creates and sells ready-to-use digital products — spreadsheets,
Notion systems, planners, and niche toolkits — through marketplaces and, later, its own
storefront. Customers are practitioners in one sharply specific niche who pay to skip
building a tool themselves.

## Why the platform is doing it

Ranked #1 on the opportunity scoreboard (85/100): near-zero marginal cost, fully
automatable delivery, starts for almost nothing, and generates cash fast. It is the
platform's cash-flow opener — its revenue funds the slower, more durable ventures, and
its content/automation toolchain is shared with the productized AI service.

## Owner and stage

- **Owner:** Troy
- **Current stage:** 1 — Validate
- **Structure:** Internal line of business

## Success looks like

- First paid sale within 30 days of the first product listing.
- 25+ total sales across at least 3 products within 90 days of first listing.
- A repeatable production pipeline: idea → build → list → promote in under a week
  per product.

## Kill / rethink criteria

- Fewer than 25 sales or under $500 total revenue within 90 days of first listing,
  across 3+ products → the niche or channel is wrong; rethink before building more.
- If two niches fail validation back-to-back → pause the venture and redirect effort
  to the productized AI service.
