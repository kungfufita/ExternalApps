# Pillar 3 — Sustainability

What keeps a venture — and the platform — viable for the long run. Sustainability here
means three things at once, and a venture is only sustainable if all three hold.

## 1. Financial sustainability

- **Unit economics first.** Every venture must know its cost to deliver one unit of
  whatever it sells, and its price, and the gap between them. If that's unknown, finding
  out is the current priority.
- **Runway is explicit.** Each venture states how many months it can operate at current
  burn, and that number appears in its quarterly review.
- **Profit funds patience.** Steady-state ventures fund the platform and give new
  ventures time to validate. That flow is tracked, not assumed.
- **No venture depends on permanent subsidy.** Support from the platform is staged and
  has an end date or a graduation trigger.

## 2. Environmental sustainability

- Prefer suppliers, materials, and processes with lower footprints when the cost is
  comparable; document the choice when it isn't.
- Design out waste before offsetting it: less packaging, less transport, less energy,
  longer-lived assets, repair over replace.
- Digital ventures are not exempt — hosting choices, data retention, and hardware
  refresh cycles are their footprint.
- Each venture lists its main environmental impacts in `operations.md` and one
  improvement it is actually pursuing. One real improvement beats ten aspirations.

## 3. Operational resilience

- **Bus factor:** any process only one person can run is flagged and gets a playbook.
- **Supplier and channel concentration:** any single supplier or channel above ~40% of
  a venture's throughput gets a named alternative.
- **Slow failure over sudden failure:** ventures keep enough margin (financial and
  operational) that trouble shows up as a trend to manage, not an event to survive.

## Cadence

Sustainability is reviewed as part of each venture's quarterly pillar review:
runway, unit economics, top environmental impact, top resilience risk — four lines,
every quarter, in `roadmap.md`.
