# Pillar 4 — Workforce

The people side of the platform: what work needs humans, how they're organized, and how
they grow. Workforce and automation (Pillar 5) are designed together — people are put
where judgment, relationships, and creativity matter; machines carry the repetition.

## Principles

- **Hire for the venture, share across the platform.** People belong to a home venture
  but skills are visible platform-wide, so a venture can borrow expertise before it
  hires for it.
- **Every role has a written outcome.** Not a task list — the result the role exists to
  produce. Task lists change; outcomes are what the role is accountable for.
- **Flexible engagement, honest classification.** Employees, contractors, and partners
  are all legitimate, but each is engaged and paid according to what they legally are.
- **Automation changes roles before it removes them.** When automation absorbs part of
  a role, the default is to move the person up the value chain — toward exceptions,
  quality, and customers — not out the door. Displacement, if it ever happens, is
  planned and humane, never a surprise.

## Structure

Each venture maintains a **role map** in its `operations.md`:

| Role | Outcome it owns | Holder | Engagement | Backup |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. Operator | Venture runs day-to-day within budget | (name) | Employee | (name) |

Rules:

- Every role has a named backup or a playbook good enough that a backup could step in.
- Split roles (one person across ventures) show their split explicitly so cost
  allocation (Pillar 1) and workload stay honest.

## Growth and development

- Each person has one development objective at a time, reviewed quarterly — a skill,
  a certification, a bigger role. One real objective beats a form full of them.
- Cross-venture moves are a feature of the platform: the second venture gets an
  experienced person, the person gets a broader career, the platform keeps the
  knowledge in-house.

## Capacity planning

- Hiring is triggered by the venture's roadmap, not by busyness: a stage exit that
  needs a capability the team lacks, or sustained overload that automation (Pillar 5)
  can't absorb.
- Before every hire, two questions in order: *Can we automate this instead?* and
  *Can we borrow this from another venture?* Hire when both answers are no.
