# BC Business Registration & Succession

Checklist for registering the business as a **sole proprietorship in British Columbia**
with **Sheila Nayler** as owner, and putting the succession intent (**Troy Nayler**)
into legally effective form.

> **Caveats.** Process and fees below are as understood in early/mid 2026 — verify
> current details at the official BC Registries pages before paying anything. The
> succession section describes intent; making it legally effective **requires a BC
> lawyer or notary**. Nothing here is legal or tax advice.

## Part 1 — Register the sole proprietorship

- [ ] **1. Choose the business name** — shortlist in
  [`naming-and-domains.md`](naming-and-domains.md). BC rule of thumb: a name needs a
  **distinctive** element + a **descriptive** element (e.g., "Bluepine" + "Digital").
  Operating under Sheila's own legal name needs no registration, but a brandable trade
  name (and matching domain) is worth the ~$70 total.
- [ ] **2. Name Request** — submit via **BC Registries Name Request Online**
  (www.bcregistry.gov.bc.ca). Fee ≈ **$30**. Submit up to 3 name choices in priority
  order. Approval typically takes a few business days; an approved Name Request is
  valid **56 days** — register the business within that window.
- [ ] **3. Register the sole proprietorship** ("registration of a DBA / firm name") —
  online through **BC Registries** using the approved Name Request. Fee ≈ **$40**.
  Registrant: **Sheila Nayler**. Keep the registration statement — the bank will want it.
- [ ] **4. Business bank account** — open in the registered name (bring the
  registration statement + Sheila's ID). Keep business money fully separate from
  personal from day one (platform Pillar 1 rule).
- [ ] **5. CRA / tax setup**
  - **GST**: registration is mandatory once revenue passes **$30,000 over four
    consecutive quarters**; voluntary before that (voluntary registration lets the
    business claim input tax credits — ask the accountant whether it's worth it now).
  - **BC PST**: BC taxes "software" broadly, and **digital products sold to BC
    customers may be PST-taxable** — confirm with an accountant whether the Deal
    Analyzer sales trigger PST registration. Marketplace sales (Etsy/Gumroad) often
    have marketplace-collected tax; confirm who remits.
  - Sole prop income is reported on **Sheila's personal T1** (form **T2125**). Get a
    bookkeeping category set up now, not at tax time.
- [ ] **6. Municipal business licence** — check the home municipality's requirement
  for a home-based online business (rules vary by city in BC).
- [ ] **7. WorkSafeBC** — not required with no employees; revisit at first hire.

## Part 2 — Succession: the business passes to Troy

**Intent:** if anything happens to Sheila, the business and everything it owns passes
to **Troy Nayler**.

**The legal reality to plan around:** a sole proprietorship is **not a separate legal
entity** — there is no share or company to "will". What passes through Sheila's estate
is the **business's assets**: the registered trade name, domains, bank balance,
marketplace/seller accounts, the IP (products, code, this repository), equipment, and
goodwill. So the mechanism is estate planning:

- [ ] **1. Will (BC lawyer or notary)** — Sheila's will should specifically gift *the
  business and all its assets* (named: trade name registration, domain names, business
  bank account, seller accounts, intellectual property, equipment) to **Troy Nayler**,
  and appoint an executor (commonly Troy). A generic "residue" clause works but a
  specific gift is cleaner for continuity.
- [ ] **2. Incapacity planning** — an **Enduring Power of Attorney** naming Troy lets
  him run the business if Sheila is alive but unable to act; a will only operates on
  death. Ask the lawyer/notary to do both in one sitting.
- [ ] **3. Practical continuity (do now, no lawyer needed)**
  - Shared **password manager** vault with all business credentials (registry account,
    domains, bank, marketplaces, email admin) accessible to both Sheila and Troy.
  - Domains and key accounts list **Troy as secondary/backup contact** where supported.
  - This repository already carries the operational knowledge — keep it that way.
- [ ] **4. Revisit at incorporation** — if the business later incorporates (see each
  venture's graduation trigger), succession switches to **shares**, which are far
  cleaner to will. This is one more argument for incorporating once revenue is real.
