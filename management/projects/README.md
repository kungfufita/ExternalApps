# Project Management

How work gets organized above the day-to-day: a **project** is any effort with a
defined outcome and an end — inside a venture (ship product #2) or at the business
level (register the company). Ongoing operations are not projects; they live in
venture `operations.md` files.

## How projects run

1. **One page per project**, copied from [`_template.md`](_template.md) into this
   directory as `P-###-short-name.md`. If it doesn't fit on a page, split it.
2. **One owner per project** — the person accountable, even if others (or automation)
   do most of the work.
3. **Statuses** are exactly one of:
   `Proposed → Active → Blocked → Done → Dropped`
   Blocked projects name their blocker and who can clear it. Dropped projects keep
   their page (retained learning, per platform rules).
4. **The register below is the truth.** A project not on it doesn't exist; the Program
   Lead keeps it current.
5. **WIP limit:** at most **3 Active projects at a time** across the whole business.
   Two people can't do more well, and the limit forces sequencing.

## Reporting

- Project owners update their one-pager and the register row when status changes.
- The Program Lead rolls material changes up into [`../status.md`](../status.md).
- A project **Done** means its outcome shipped — not that its tasks were attempted.
